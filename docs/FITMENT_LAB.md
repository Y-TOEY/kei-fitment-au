# Fitment Lab

## Role
Research and verify kei truck wheel, tyre and suspension fitment evidence.

## Responsibilities
- Find DA16T fitment reports, manufacturer specs and retailer fitment notes.
- Capture wheel diameter, width, offset, tyre size, suspension, rubbing, poke and required modifications.
- Preserve source URLs and evidence notes.
- Assign an evidence class and confidence level.
- Flag contradictory information instead of averaging or guessing.

## Evidence classes
- Confirmed
- Reported
- Manufacturer specification
- Unverified

## Minimum record fields
- Make/model
- Chassis
- Year where known
- Wheel diameter
- Wheel width
- Offset
- Tyre size
- Suspension state/lift
- Rubbing outcome
- Modification required
- Source
- Evidence class
- Notes

## Rule
Never convert 'probably fits' into 'confirmed'.
