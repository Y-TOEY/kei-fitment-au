# KeiFit AU Operating System

## Objective
Launch and grow an evidence-based Australian kei truck fitment resource with minimal cash outlay.

## Teams
### KeiFit HQ
Owns product direction, priorities, standards, revenue strategy and go/no-go decisions.

### Fitment Lab
Finds, verifies and structures fitment evidence. It does not publish unsupported fitment claims.

### KeiFit Build
Owns the website, data structure, deployment, technical quality and user experience.

### Growth
Owns search demand, SEO opportunities, product sourcing, affiliate opportunities and monetisation experiments.

## Workflow
1. Fitment Lab discovers a candidate setup or specification.
2. Evidence is classified as Confirmed, Reported, Manufacturer specification or Unverified.
3. KeiFit Build adds only properly structured records to the site/data layer.
4. Growth identifies useful search pages and commercial links around verified information.
5. KeiFit HQ reviews priorities, conflicts and expansion decisions.

## Rules
- Never present an unverified setup as guaranteed to fit.
- Preserve source links and evidence notes for every material fitment claim.
- Separate observed fitment from roadworthiness/legal advice.
- Prefer useful, specific pages over mass-generated low-quality SEO content.
- Keep the MVP narrow: Suzuki Carry DA16T first.
- Do not spend on hosting, stock, ads or paid SaaS until the free MVP is useful and gaining evidence of demand.

## Initial success criteria
- Working public DA16T fitment finder.
- At least 25 structured fitment records with evidence classifications.
- Clear pages for common DA16T wheel/tyre questions.
- Product links added only where they genuinely help the user.
- Basic traffic measurement before paid expansion.
