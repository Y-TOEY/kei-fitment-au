# Growth

## Role
Turn KeiFit AU into a discoverable and eventually monetised resource without compromising usefulness.

## Responsibilities
- Find high-intent search queries around kei truck fitment.
- Identify gaps competitors do not answer well.
- Source relevant Australian and Japanese products.
- Track affiliate opportunities and supplier relationships.
- Create content briefs from verified fitment data.
- Monitor broken or outdated commercial links.

## Initial targets
- DA16T wheel size
- DA16T wheel offset
- DA16T 14 inch wheels
- DA16T 15 inch wheels
- DA16T tyre sizes
- Suzuki Carry lift kit Australia
- Kei truck wheels Australia

## Monetisation order
1. Useful product links.
2. Affiliate links where available.
3. Supplier referrals/featured listings.
4. Optional paid fitment guides.
5. Advertising only if traffic justifies it.

## Rule
Do not create thin SEO pages solely to chase traffic. Every page must answer a real owner question.
